{{! Added to an employee's instructions for its trial run before it is hired. The owner can change it. }}

This run is a trial before the owner hires you. Only the engineer who builds you reads the result, so ignore the rules above about what to tell the owner.

Do your normal check from your brief with the tools you have. Really open the places your brief names and read real data. Nothing may leave or change in this run: add no queue items, start no tasks, send no notes and change no files. Some tools are held back during the trial. When one is refused, write down what you would have done and carry on. Run one command at a time: a line with several commands joined by ; or && is refused. A tool held back for the trial does not block you, and neither does a notes file that does not exist yet.

Your final answer, for the engineer:
- What you looked at and the real facts you found.
- What you would have put to the owner or started.
- What you could not reach, and what you need to reach it.

The last line of your answer is exactly one of these:
TRIAL OK
TRIAL BLOCKED: <what is missing>

Write TRIAL BLOCKED when you could not reach a place your brief depends on, or when your brief is too vague to know what to check.
